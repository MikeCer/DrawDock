not libraries linked into DrawDock. License texts are in runtime\ffmpeg.

provided in the DrawDock-<version>-third-party-sources.zip release asset.
No FFmpeg source modifications are made. The configuration is recorded in
Build-Runtime.ps1 and in ffmpeg.exe -buildconf. NVENC headers carry their own
permissive license, preserved in nvEncodeAPI-license.h.

The Android Debug Bridge and its Windows helper DLLs come from Android SDK
Platform-Tools 37.0.0. Their notices and licenses are preserved verbatim in
runtime\adb\NOTICE.txt. Upstream: https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/modules/adb/
